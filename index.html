Medical coding automation

A multi-agent LLM system for revenue cycle management. Each coding module, from CPT and ICD to NCCI edits and MIPS, runs as its own agent with its own prompts and retrieval.

How it works
  • Architecture: LangChain and LangGraph multi-agent graph with agent-specific prompts.
  • Retrieval: semantic search over CPT and ICD codes in Pinecone and Qdrant, plus PostgreSQL.
  • Knowledge: LCD/NCD article matching, NCCI edits and MIPS measures as retrievable sources.
  • Fine-tuning: LLaMA 3.3 with LoRA for extraction and diagnosis prediction.
  • Accuracy: CPT 88%, modifier 86%, MIPS 100%, ICD 70%.

ZenBuild: AI architectural design

An end-to-end platform that generates 2D residential floorplans. It extends the DeepLayout framework, trained on RPLAN, with a four-stage neural pipeline and a vectorizer that turns pixels into clean vector plans.

How it works
  • Sequential modules: living room, continue, walls, then room locations.
  • Up to 99.5% validation accuracy across the four modules.
  • About 8 seconds per design versus roughly 45 minutes of manual MATLAB drafting.
  • Single-story homes of 60–120 m² with 2, 3 or 4 rooms; a Flask app renders rooms with realistic textures.

Computer vision, open source

Bone fracture detection

A two-stage CNN pipeline for upper-limb X-rays. A ResNet-50 router identifies hand, shoulder or elbow, then a specialist ResNet-50 for that body part decides fractured or normal.

How it works
  • Trained on Stanford’s MURA dataset of 20,335 musculoskeletal radiographs.
  • Horizontal-flip augmentation and a 72/18/10 train, validation and test split.
  • Adam optimizer at learning rate 1e-4 with early stopping.
  • Ships as a CustomTkinter desktop app with an optional Flask web demo.

Computer vision, open source

Fine-grained bird classification

A benchmark of four configurations on CUB-200-2011 (200 species): a ResNet-50 baseline, bounding-box crops, MixUp/CutMix, and a two-phase EfficientNet-B4. Grad-CAM confirms the model looks at the bird, not the background.

How it works
  • Stratified 70/15/15 split across 200 species.
  • ResNet-50 baseline: 82.18% top-1 and 95.93% top-5 on validation.
  • EfficientNet-B4 with warm-up, full fine-tuning and label smoothing was the strongest backbone overall.
  • Reproducible scripts for data, training, evaluation, Grad-CAM and checkpointing.
